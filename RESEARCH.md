# Website research and design decisions

Updated on the Test branch, 8 October 2026.

## Sources

- Mantrapro Company Profile: assets/mantrapro-company-profile.pdf. Authoritative source for company history, leadership, registration, services, values, addresses and strategic objectives.
- Dalberg, What We Do: https://dalberg.com/what-we-do/ (reviewed 8 October 2026). Describes capabilities individually and identifies the types of organisations they serve.
- Genesis Analytics, What We Do: https://www.genesis-analytics.com/what-we-do (search-index text reviewed 8 October 2026; direct retrieval unavailable). Presents distinct advisory practices with explanations of scope and context.

## Application to Mantrapro

These are design conclusions drawn from the research, rather than claims made by the reference firms about Mantrapro.

- Separate Home, Company, Services, Approach and Contact pages, with the same navigation on each page.
- Plain service names, intended client context and concise descriptions of the work.
- Company facts and named leadership rather than invented performance figures, client logos or testimonials.
- Distinguish current South African service coverage from plans to develop partnerships across Africa.
- Use restrained typography, navy, white and muted gold, conventional page layouts, and the supplied profile photograph.
- Keep CSS and JavaScript in external files. All content and navigation remain available without JavaScript.
- Direct email and phone contact, verified office details and the supplied PDF. Service enquiry links prefill an email subject; no form submission or backend is implied.

## Verification limits

Local file references, page markup, JavaScript syntax and navigation behaviour were checked in code. No browser screenshots were captured, so visual rendering and device-level testing are not claimed.
