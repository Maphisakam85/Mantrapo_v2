Established in 1997, Mantrapro (Pty) Ltd is a South African enterprise development, business consulting and training company headquartered in the Free State.

We support entrepreneurs, private companies, investors, government institutions and development organisations. Our work strengthens governance, improves operational efficiency and prepares commercially viable projects for investment.

Our approach combines international business standards with an understanding of the economic realities of African markets.
