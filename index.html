Background

A South African consultancy built for African markets

Mantrapro (Pty) Ltd is a South African enterprise development and training business consulting company established in 1997. We support sustainable economic development by assisting entrepreneurs, private companies, investors, government institutions and development organisations throughout the African continent.

Through our consulting expertise, we strengthen governance, improve operational efficiency, prepare investment-ready projects and facilitate strategic partnerships that generate measurable business value — built on international standards, and responsive to the realities of African markets.

Ownership

Founder-led, privately owned and accountable

Mantrapro (Pty) Ltd is a privately owned South African company founded by Mr. Johannes Moshe in 1997. The company operates under a single-shareholder ownership structure, with Mr. Johannes Moshe holding 100% of the issued shares and serving as the Sole Director.

This structure supports clear strategic leadership, efficient decision-making and consistent implementation of the company's long-term objectives. Mr. Moshe remains actively involved in strategic planning, client engagement, investment facilitation and business expansion initiatives.

The ownership model reflects Mantrapro's commitment to accountability, corporate governance and long-term business sustainability while supporting its vision of inclusive economic development throughout Africa.

Corporate governance

Sound corporate governance is fundamental to sustainable business success, investor confidence and long-term value creation. We conduct our operations with integrity, transparency, accountability, fairness and ethical leadership.

Governance principles are embedded throughout our organisation to ensure responsible decision-making, effective risk management, and compliance with applicable legislation — protecting stakeholder interests as we continue to expand across the African continent, in line with internationally recognised standards.

Competitive advantages

Mantrapro combines enterprise development consulting, investment facilitation, financial brokerage, M&A advisory, project preparation and operational improvement under one organisation — supporting clients through the complete business lifecycle, from concept and feasibility through to funding, implementation, optimisation, expansion and exit.

Few consulting firms in Africa offer this breadth within a single organisation. That strength is reinforced by decades of leadership experience, strong stakeholder relationships and a practical, implementable approach — built on People, Product and Process — rather than theoretical advice.
