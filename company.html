About Mantrapro

An enterprise development, business consulting and training company established in South Africa in 1997.

Company background

Mantrapro assists organisations in strengthening governance, improving operational efficiency, preparing investment-ready projects and facilitating strategic partnerships.

We work with entrepreneurs, private companies, investors, government institutions and development organisations. Our approach draws on international business standards while responding to the economic realities of African markets.

Ownership & leadership

The company was founded by Mr. Johannes Moshe, who holds 100% of the issued shares and serves as Sole Director. He remains involved in strategic planning, client engagement, investment facilitation and business expansion.

Geographic focus

Headquartered in the Free State, Mantrapro provides services throughout South Africa. The company's expansion strategy focuses on developing partnerships across Southern, East, West, Central and North Africa.
